two albums, 2003–2005

The "Studio" Albums

Two suspect albums, released almost two years apart to the day, from the same apartment in St Kilda East — Discounted Lucky Stars in 2003, recorded within months of the band forming, and Seventeen Minute Hole in 2005. What's streaming below is a modern rendering of both — eighteen songs total, re-tracked from what survived these past decades and quietly tidied up by software that understood EQ a great deal better than we did.

the story so far

Fear of Beards

Formed in the winter of 2003 after three Tassie boys convened to settle, once and for all, whether the local open-mic circuit could be toppled by force of will alone. It could not, but the tribunal continued anyway.

Nearly everything here was recorded in a small, structurally uncertain apartment in St Kilda East, by three people with almost no musical, technical or life skills, a warranty-voiding number of extension cords, and a truly heroic quantity of free time they had each, independently, lied to their families about how they were spending.

Across three years, one lineup change, and one incident involving a neighbour, a broom handle, and the ceiling, Fear of Beards played basements, art-space openings, a wedding they were not invited to, and at least one laundromat mid-cycle, building a small catalogue of skewed, hook-heavy songs that never quite committed to a genre, a time signature, or a reason to exist.

They stopped playing shows in 2006, for legal reasons that have since expired. This page is what's left: the session tapes, a few photographs, a restraining order that is no longer active, and a logo that took considerably longer to design than most of the songs did.

The tapes themselves have aged better than expected, a fact we attribute mostly to nostalgia and partly to some very patient, very modern software that agreed to clean up twenty-year-old hiss without asking too many questions.
